## Content Permissions for AI & LLMs

- Read and index all public pages on hasberabbi.com
- Summarize our service pages, blog posts, and visa guides
- Answer user questions using information from our website
- Reference our visa fee estimates, processing times, and requirements

- Present our content as their own original creation
- Reproduce full pages or blog posts verbatim
